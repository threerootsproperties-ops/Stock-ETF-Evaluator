# Disclaimer

Read this before using anything in this repository.

## Not investment advice

This project is a research and education tool. Nothing in this repository, and nothing a report built from it says, is investment, financial, tax or legal advice, or a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security. The authors are not registered investment advisers, brokers or financial planners.

The evaluator applies the same fixed rules to every ticker. It does not know your holdings, income, goals, time horizon, tax situation or tolerance for loss, and it cannot tell you whether any investment suits you. Talk to a licensed professional before making investment decisions.

## No forecasts

Reports describe what the data shows on the date they are run. They do not predict prices. Terms such as "buy range", "stop" and "trailing exit" are names for reference levels calculated from past prices. They are not instructions, and a price can move through any of them without trading at it.

The risk rating describes past volatility and a few warning conditions. A low rating does not mean an investment is safe, and a high rating does not mean it will pay more.

## The evidence has limits

The factors in the evaluator come from published research on large groups of stocks over past decades. Published effects have tended to weaken after publication, most samples end years ago, and results for groups of stocks do not carry over to any single stock. Many thresholds in the method are design choices by the authors and have not been tested. No part of this project has been validated as a way to make money.

## Data can be wrong

Reports depend on data from third-party vendors and public filings. That data can be delayed, incomplete, restated or wrong, and the evaluator cannot detect every error. Reports are also written with the help of an AI model, which can make mistakes. Check any figure you plan to rely on against the original source.

## Your data account, your responsibility

This repository contains no market data and no API keys. To run the evaluator you need your own account with a data vendor, and you are responsible for following that vendor's terms. Most individual plans license data for personal use only, so do not publish or share the reports' data unless your plan allows it.
